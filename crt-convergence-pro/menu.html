About CRT Convergence

CRT convergence refers to the alignment of the red, green, and blue electron beams on the screen. Proper convergence ensures sharp, clear images without color fringing. Use the RGB test patterns to check and adjust your CRT monitor's convergence settings.

What to look for: The RGB lines and dots should appear as single white lines/dots. If you see separate red, green, and blue lines, your monitor needs convergence adjustment using the service menu or internal controls.
